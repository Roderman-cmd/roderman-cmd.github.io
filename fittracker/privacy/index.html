Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

FitTracker is a weightlifting log for iPhone. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and nothing you log is sent to us. This policy explains what the app stores, where it's kept, and what you control. “We” means FitTracker's developer.

In short

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics or other third-party code in the app.
  • Your workouts are stored on your iPhone and, if iCloud Sync is on, in your own private iCloud. We can't see them.
  • Apple Health data is used only for the features you turn on. It's never used for advertising, sold or shared.
  • We only receive what you choose to send us through the support form.

What FitTracker stores

Everything you enter, on your iPhone:

  • Training: exercises, and sets with their weight, reps, partial reps, RPE, tags, notes and machine or brand. For bodyweight exercises, a set also keeps the bodyweight it was counted at. Cardio time, distance, calories, incline and resistance. Workout templates and day types.
  • Body: the bodyweight you log, body measurements and progress photos.
  • Settings: such as your units, rest timer, weekly goal and reminders. These stay on your iPhone.

iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is on unless you turn it off. It keeps your FitTracker data in the private database of your iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit), so it's backed up and appears on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Only your Apple Account can access it; we can't. Apple stores it under Apple's privacy policy.

You can turn it off in FitTracker's Settings → iCloud Sync. Your data then stays on this iPhone only. Turning it off doesn't delete anything.

Apple Health

FitTracker uses Apple Health only with your permission, and only for what you turn on in Settings → Apple Health:

  • Reads your body weight, so bodyweight exercises like pull-ups and dips count it toward your volume.
  • Saves your gym sessions as workouts (with the calories and distance you log for cardio), and the body weight you log.

Weigh-ins read from Apple Health are kept in a separate store on your iPhone that is never synced to iCloud. FitTracker never uses Health data for advertising or marketing, never sells it and never shares it with anyone. You can change what FitTracker may read or save at any time in the Health app.

Progress photos

Photos you take with the camera or choose from your library are stored inside FitTracker, not in your photo library. Before a photo is saved, its location and other metadata are removed. Photos sync with iCloud like the rest of your data when iCloud Sync is on.

The Face ID lock is handled by iOS. FitTracker only learns whether unlocking worked, and never has access to your face data.

Notifications, widgets and Live Activities

Rest timer alerts and reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; no server sends them. Widgets and the rest timer's Live Activity read a small summary the app keeps on your iPhone.

Files you import or export

Importing reads only the file you choose. Exporting creates a file and shares it where you choose. Rest timer sounds you add from Files are copied into the app.

What we receive

  • Messages you send us. If you write to us through the support form, we receive your email address, your message and anything else you include. We use it only to answer you and to improve FitTracker, and we delete it if you ask. The form is delivered to us by Formspree, a form service.
  • From Apple. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple gives us crash reports and app usage statistics that don't identify you. App Store ratings and reviews are public.

What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling of data, no account, and no analytics or third-party code in the app. The app doesn't send your data to any server of ours.

Deleting your data

  • Delete anything in the app. With iCloud Sync on, it's deleted from iCloud too.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy as well, go to your iPhone's Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, and delete FitTracker's data there.
  • Workouts and weights FitTracker saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Children

FitTracker isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't collect personal data from anyone.

Your rights

We don't hold your FitTracker data, so there's nothing on our side to access, correct or delete, apart from messages you've sent us. To ask about those, or anything else in this policy, use the support form. We answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
